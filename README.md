## How We Deliver Shopify Projects

Our eCommerce development process is designed for transparency — you see progress at every stage.

| Stage | What happens | Timeline |
|---|---|---|
| **Discovery** | We audit your store, analyze competitors, and define scope. You receive a project brief with timeline, milestones, and cost estimate before work begins. | 1–2 weeks |
| **Design** | Wireframes in Figma, followed by high-fidelity designs. You review and approve every page before development starts. | 2–4 weeks |
| **Development** | Clean, modular Liquid code. Git version control, Jira for task tracking. Full visibility into progress through weekly demos. | 4–8 weeks |
| **Launch & Optimize** | Deployment, QA, redirect setup, and post-launch monitoring. After launch, we analyze real user data and optimize for conversion. | Ongoing |

Engagement models: [On-Demand Service](https://mgroupweb.com/on-demand-shopify-retainer/) · [Time & Material](https://mgroupweb.com/time-and-material-shopify-development/) · [Fixed Price](https://mgroupweb.com/fixed-price-shopify-development/)
